Questions

What gets uploaded?

Nothing from your computer. A demo starts with an empty workspace, and the agent sees only what you type to it.

Which agents and models work?

Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi and Hermes each finished the test above. The public demo offers all five: OpenCode, Pi and Hermes on open Gemma models, and Claude Code and Codex on DeepSeek Flash. A deployment of your own runs them on the models your organization connects.

What happens when the demo ends?

The organization, its workspaces and everything in them are removed. We keep the name, email and company you gave so that we can follow up about the demo, and nothing else.

Who can see my workspace and runs?

A demo organization is yours alone, on an address of its own. Within an organization, administrators see people, spend and the record of runs.
