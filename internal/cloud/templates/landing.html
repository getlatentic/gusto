Questions

What gets uploaded?

Nothing from your computer. A demo starts with an empty workspace, and the agent sees only what you type to it.

Which harnesses and models work?

Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi and Hermes each finished the test above, on the models your organisation connects. The public demo offers OpenCode, Pi and Hermes on open models.

What happens when the demo ends?

The organisation, its workspaces and everything in them are removed. We keep the name, email and company you gave so that we can follow up about the demo, and nothing else.

Who can see my workspace and runs?

A demo organisation is yours alone, on an address of its own. Within an organisation, administrators see people, spend and the record of runs.
