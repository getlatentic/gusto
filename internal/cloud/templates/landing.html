Agents are becoming part of the workforce.

They need more than access to a model.

Teams are adopting coding agents, research agents, internal agents and autonomous workflows. But every new agent introduces another set of models, credentials, permissions, computers, network access, costs and logs to manage.

Latentic Agent Cloud gives your organisation a common environment for running them.

  • Agents and models your organisation approves.
  • Secure places for them to work.
  • Controls over what they can access.
  • A record of what they did.

One place for your organisation's agents.

  • Approved agents and models

    Decide which agents, harnesses and models are available to your teams. Give different people and teams access to different capabilities without managing every AI tool separately.

    Your agents. Your models. Your choice.

  • Secure computers for agent work

    Agents do more than generate text. They read files, write code, run software, use tools and interact with systems. Give them isolated computers where they can perform that work without unrestricted access to the rest of your organisation.

    Each person's work remains isolated, with access opened only where you allow it.

  • Control what agents can reach

    Define what an agent can use before it starts working. Control access to:

    • models
    • tools
    • files and repositories
    • networks
    • internal systems
    • credentials
    • external services

    Credentials remain outside the agent's environment and can be provided only when needed.

  • Control usage and spend

    Set limits at the organisation, team, user or job level. Choose which models people may use, how much work can run, and how much it may cost. See usage across your organisation from one place.

  • Know what happened

    Agent work should not disappear into a chat history. Keep a record of:

    • who started the work
    • which agent ran
    • which model it used
    • what was requested
    • what it accessed
    • what it changed
    • what it cost

    Create an auditable history of agent activity across your organisation.

From agent request to controlled execution.

  1. Your organisation decides what is available

    Administrators approve agents, models and access policies.
