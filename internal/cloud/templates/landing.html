What the demo shows

  1. Start

    Give a name, a work email and your company. You land in an organisation of your own, signed in. The first demo of the hour takes a few minutes while a machine starts, and your time begins when it is ready.

  2. Ask an agent

    Start an agent in your computer, pick a model, and ask it for something. It works in a sandbox that can reach the models and nothing else you have not allowed.

  3. See the admin side

    Open the console: people and roles, what has been spent, which models are allowed and what each costs, and who may use which.

What you control

  • A computer for each person

    Every person's agents run in a virtual machine of their own. One person's work cannot reach another's.

  • Your models, your keys

    Choose the models your organisation may use and what each costs. Keys stay outside the sandbox, added on the way out and never shown to an agent.

  • Limits that hold

    Cap what a job or a team may spend, limit a person to some models, and set how many computers and jobs an organisation may run.

  • A record you can check

    Every job leaves a signed record of what was asked, what ran and what it cost, which anybody can verify later.

See it on your own work

Thirty minutes is enough to start an agent on a real task. If it fits, a call is the next step.
