{{template "ui-chev"}} How this works

Each person signs in to an app that is on from Connections, with their own account there. Their agents then call it as them, through the gateway: the agent never holds the token, and sees what that person can see in the app, no more. In a room, an agent uses a person's apps only for what that person asks, and only after they let it in that room. Turning an app off stops it from the next task. An app that needs setup is registered by whoever runs this server, with its client id in GUSTO_CONNECTOR_CLIENTS.
