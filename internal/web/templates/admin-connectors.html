{{template "ui-chev"}} How this works

Turn on an app, and each person may sign in to it from Connections, with their own account there. Their agents then call it as them, through the gateway: the agent never holds the token, and sees what that person can see in the app, no more. A room's agent is given nobody's sign-ins. Turning an app off stops it from the next task.
